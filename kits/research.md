# Kit: research

Research questions, business ideas, market checks, reports and decks. Usually no code.

## Agents

None needed by default. Optional, from Agency Agents (copied into `.claude/agents/`):
`product/product-trend-researcher.md`, `product/product-feedback-synthesizer.md`,
`research/research-synthesist.md`.

## Plugins and skills (see EXTRAS.md)

- **Optional:** `last30days` (what people said about a topic recently), `diagram-design` (clear
  diagrams). Claude's built-in document skills (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, PDF) and deep research,
  where the app has them.

## Connectors

Web search and fetch are built in. Google Drive, Notion or Claude's own docs for sharing the
result, if the owner uses them.

## Self-check

Every claim in the report has a source link that was opened and says what the report says.
Numbers are re-checked against their source. Anything uncertain is marked as uncertain.

## Preview

The report in `.claude/hub/research/<topic>.md` (or the project folder), shared as a document, a
deck or a page.

## Hot files and resources

None.

## Hands-on (not approved from evidence alone)

Anything sent to other people, posted publicly, or that spends money.
